Research at IISER Thiruvananthapuram

Under Professor. Krishnendu Gope (Head of Ultrafast Molecular Dynamics Laboratory)

Classical and Semiclassical modelling of Dissociation Dynamics of Ozone molecule

Brief Summary

The ozone molecule (O3) is of significant physical interest due to its natural abundance. In this work, we model the Dissociative Electron Attachment (DEA) process classically and then semiclassically.

Because full quantum treatment of O3 is complex, we begin with a simplified triatomic model: a bent linear chain.

Ozone exhibits two major DEA channels: (1) a symmetric breakup, where O2 dominates vibrational modes, and (2) an antisymmetric breakup, where O2− dominates rotational modes.
